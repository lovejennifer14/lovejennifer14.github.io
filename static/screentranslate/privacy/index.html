Privacy Policy: Screen Translate and Screen Translator Lite

Effective date: October 2026 Last updated: October 2026

This policy covers two Android apps published on Google Play by WTAO LLC (developer name on Google Play: Lomoware):

  • Screen Translate: Manga, Games (com.lomoware.screen_translate), a paid app ($2.99)
  • Screen Translator Lite: OCR (com.lomoware.screen_translate.lite), a free app with an optional one-time in-app purchase (pro_unlock)

In this policy, "the app" means either app, and "we" or "us" means WTAO LLC. Where the two apps differ, we say so.

This page is published at https://wtao.top/screentranslate/privacy. It matches what we declare in the Data safety section of each app's Google Play listing.

The short version

  • Translation on your phone stays on your phone. In Quick and AI Enhanced modes, text recognition (OCR) and translation run on your device. Your screen content and screenshots are not sent anywhere.
  • Cloud AI mode is optional. It's available in both apps. If you turn it on and enter your own Zhipu AI API key, the recognized text is sent to Zhipu AI (in China) to be translated. We don't receive or store that text.
  • Quick and AI Enhanced modes run entirely on your device; only Cloud AI mode sends text off the device.
  • We use Google Firebase for usage analytics and crash reports. This includes app-install identifiers and an approximate location worked out from your IP address. Version 1.2.5 of the paid app can also access the Android Advertising ID through Firebase. We use it only for analytics, never for ads, and the next update removes it. Lite does not access it.
  • No ads, no accounts, and we don't sell your data. We don't use your data for advertising.
  • You can ask us to delete your data by emailing support@wtao.top. See How to request deletion of your data.

1. What stays on your device

  • Your screen content. The app needs to see what's on your screen to translate it. The screen text, and any screen images the app captures, are processed on your device and never leave it. Not to us, and not to anyone else. The only exception is Cloud AI mode, if you turn it on: then the recognized text (not the image) is sent to Zhipu AI (see section 2).
  • Translations in Quick and AI Enhanced modes. Text recognition and translation run on your device. The text-recognition model is built into the app. Language packs are downloaded once from Hugging Face (huggingface.co), and some models are downloaded from Google through ML Kit. Those downloads don't include any of your content.
  • Your settings, such as language choices and modes.
  • Your Pro unlock status (Lite). It is checked on your device and stored there.
  • Your Zhipu API key (only if you use Cloud AI mode). It is stored only on your device. It is sent only to Zhipu, with each Cloud AI mode request, so Zhipu can process the request under your own Zhipu account. We never receive it.

No user accounts. You don't sign up or log in to use either app, and we don't ask for your name, email address or phone number. The optional Zhipu API key belongs to your own account with Zhipu AI. It is not an account with us.

3. How we use data

We use the data above only to:

  • Make the app work: translate text in Cloud AI mode when you ask for it, deliver app settings through Remote Config, and unlock Pro in Lite.
  • Understand how the app is used, in aggregate, so we can decide what to improve.
  • Find and fix crashes and bugs.
  • Meet legal obligations, such as tax records for purchases.

We don't use your data for advertising or marketing, we don't build advertising profiles, and the apps show no ads.

5. Sharing

  • We don't sell your personal information. We don't "share" it for cross-context behavioral advertising, as the CCPA uses that term.
  • Google (Firebase) processes analytics, crash and diagnostic data on our behalf, as our service provider (a "processor" under the GDPR). How our Google settings are configured:
    • Google signals is off in Google Analytics.
    • No ad products are linked: Google Analytics and Firebase aren't linked to Google Ads, AdMob or any other Google ad product.
    • Data sharing: "Google products & services" and "Modeling contributions" are off.
    • Google's access: Google may access the data only to give us technical support and to provide recommendations.
  • Zhipu AI. When you use Cloud AI mode in either app, the recognized text is shared with Zhipu AI, a third party, so it can translate it. The request uses your own API key, under your own Zhipu account. Zhipu's terms and privacy policy apply to that text, including how long Zhipu keeps it. The text goes straight from your device to Zhipu. We don't receive it or store it on our servers. If you don't want text sent to Zhipu, leave Cloud AI mode off.
  • Legal reasons. We may disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect our rights or users' safety.
  • Business transfers. If WTAO LLC is sold or merges with another company, this policy will continue to apply to data collected under it.

International transfers. Google and Hugging Face may process data in the United States and other countries. Cloud AI mode text is sent to Zhipu AI in China. Data protection laws in those countries may differ from those where you live.

6. How long data is kept

  • Firebase Analytics: event data is kept for 2 months. User-level data is kept for up to 14 months after your last activity in the app, and new activity restarts that period. Aggregated reports that don't identify you may be kept longer.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: crash traces and their IDs are kept for 90 days, after which Google begins deleting them.
  • Firebase installation IDs (used by Remote Config) are kept until they are deleted, for example when you make a deletion request.
  • Purchase records: Google Play keeps them under Google's policies. We keep order records only as long as tax and accounting law requires.
  • Cloud AI mode text: we don't keep it. Zhipu keeps it according to its own policy.
  • Data on your device stays until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.
  • Emails you send us are kept as long as we need them to handle your request.

7. How to request deletion of your data

By email. You can email support@wtao.top to ask us to delete any data we hold about you. Send it from the address you want us to reply to, with the subject "Data deletion request", and tell us which app you use: Screen Translate or Screen Translator Lite.

WTAO LLC processes requests within 30 days, and we reply to tell you what we did:

  • Analytics data is deleted through Google Analytics' user deletion.
  • Crash reports are deleted automatically after 90 days.

We may need to keep some records, such as purchase records required for tax purposes. You don't need an account with us to make a request.

A standalone version of these instructions is at https://wtao.top/screentranslate/delete-data.

On your device: - Clear app data: go to Android Settings → Apps → [app name] → Storage → Clear data. This deletes your settings and your saved Zhipu API key from the device. It also resets the app's Firebase IDs, so new analytics data won't be linked to your old data. In Lite, you can restore Pro afterwards through Google Play. - Uninstall the app: this removes everything the app stored on your device and stops all further collection. - Leave Cloud AI mode off, or remove your API key, to stop text being sent to Zhipu.

Clearing data or uninstalling doesn't delete data that has already been sent to Firebase. That data is deleted at the end of the retention periods in section 6, or sooner if you email us. To delete data held by Zhipu, use the options in Zhipu's privacy policy.

9. Children

The apps are for people aged 13 and older. They are not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you think a child under 13 has used the app and data was collected, email support@wtao.top and we will delete it.

10. Security

  • Everything the app sends or downloads goes over encrypted connections (HTTPS). That covers Firebase, Zhipu AI (open.bigmodel.cn) in Cloud AI mode, language packs from Hugging Face, and ML Kit model downloads from Google.
  • Access to our Firebase and Google Play consoles is limited to the people who need it.

No system is perfectly secure, but we keep the data we handle to a minimum.
