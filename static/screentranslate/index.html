Questions

Why does it ask for screen-capture permission?

Android requires that permission for any app that reads on-screen text. The app explains this before anything is captured, and in Quick mode the text never leaves your device.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Quick mode downloads a language pack once, then translates entirely on-device — on a plane, abroad, anywhere.

Which apps does it work with?

Any app that shows text: manga and webtoon readers, mobile games, chat and social apps, browsers.

Why isn't it free with ads?

Because software you buy once is better than software that rents itself back to you. $4.99 once is less than a single month of most rival apps' subscription plans.
