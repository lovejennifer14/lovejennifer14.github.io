学校简介

About our school

“文韬”出自《六韬》——文采与韬略并重。我们取这个名字，是希望孩子学中文不只是认字、考试， 而是能读书、会表达，慢慢把中文变成自己的本事。

Why we teach · 为什么教中文

给华裔家庭的孩子For children from Chinese-speaking families

Chinese is the language of home — the language children speak with their grandparents, and the root of their culture. We want our students to do more than “understand it when they hear it”: we want them to read it, write it and express themselves in it, so Chinese truly becomes their own skill, passed down from generation to generation.

中文是家里的语言，是孩子和爷爷奶奶、外公外婆说话的语言，也是他们文化里的根。 我们希望孩子不只是“听得懂”，而是能读、能写、能表达，把中文真正变成自己的本事， 一代一代传下去。

给对中文感兴趣的孩子For children curious about Chinese

For a child with no Chinese background, Chinese is a brand-new door opening. We start from interest — making Chinese feel fun, so children dare to open their mouths and speak — and through conversation and exchange, time after time, they gradually fall in love with the language and the culture behind it.

对没有中文背景的孩子来说，中文是一扇新打开的门。我们从兴趣出发，让孩子先觉得 中文好玩、敢开口说，在一次次对话和交流里，慢慢爱上这门语言和它背后的文化。

How we teach · 我们怎么教

华裔系统班The heritage track

We use China’s 部编教材 — the national Ministry-edited textbooks — progressing systematically from Level 1 through Level 6 across all four skills: listening, speaking, reading and writing. Completing the full sequence gives children a solid foundation for the AP Chinese exam and the HSK Chinese proficiency test later on.

使用中国部编教材，从 Level 1 一直到 Level 6，按听说读写四个方面系统推进、 稳步升级。学完整个体系，孩子就为以后的 AP 中文考试和 HSK 汉语水平考试 打下了坚实的基础。

兴趣启蒙班The interest-based track

We use Easy Steps to Chinese （轻松学中文）, together with supplementary materials chosen for each child. In class we encourage children to speak up — lots of conversation, dialogue and exchange — in a relaxed atmosphere where interest comes first, and skills grow step by step from there.

使用《轻松学中文》以及针对孩子特点准备的补充教材。课堂上鼓励孩子开口， 多对话、多交流，在轻松的氛围里先对中文产生兴趣，再一步步学下去。

认识笔画

Try it: the basic strokes

Every Chinese character is built from a handful of basic strokes. Tap one to watch how it's written — this is exactly how we introduce them in class.

横héng

horizontal stroke

课堂小知识 · In class we call each stroke by its Chinese name from day one. English is only a first-day bridge — textbooks, worksheets and teachers everywhere use 横、竖、撇、捺， so learning the Chinese names first saves children from re-learning later.
