ScreenTranslate — Delete Your Data

This page explains how to ask WTAO LLC (developer name on Google Play: Lomoware) to delete data related to these Android apps:

  • Screen Translate: Manga, Games (com.lomoware.screen_translate)
  • Screen Translator Lite: OCR (com.lomoware.screen_translate.lite)

For full details, see our Privacy Policy.

How to request deletion

  1. Email support@wtao.top from the address you want us to reply to.
  2. Use the subject "Data deletion request".
  3. Tell us which app you use: Screen Translate or Screen Translator Lite.

You don't need an account with us, and you don't need to send us any device or Firebase ID. WTAO LLC processes requests within 30 days and replies to tell you what we did.

What data we hold

The apps have no user accounts. We don't ask for your name, email address or phone number, and we don't store the text on your screen, your translations or your screenshots.

The data that leaves your device is handled by Google Firebase on our behalf:

  • Firebase Analytics: usage events (screens, sessions, language, mode, counts, durations, error types and, in Lite, purchase events), diagnostics and an approximate location worked out from your IP address. Events are tied to random IDs created for each app install, not to your name.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: crash logs and diagnostic details (device model, OS version, app version and similar), tied to a random installation ID.
  • Paid app version 1.2.5 only: the Android Advertising ID may be accessed through Firebase, for analytics only. Lite does not access it.

What is deleted and when

  • Analytics data is deleted through Google Analytics' user deletion, processed within 30 days of your request.
  • Crash reports are deleted automatically by Google after 90 days.
  • Firebase installation IDs are kept until they are deleted, for example when you make a deletion request.
  • Kept: we may need to keep some records, such as order records required for tax and accounting purposes.

Cloud AI mode (Zhipu AI)

Cloud AI mode is optional. Only if you turn it on and enter your own Zhipu AI (BigModel, open.bigmodel.cn) API key is the recognized text sent from your device to Zhipu AI to be translated. We don't receive or store that text. Zhipu's own privacy policy applies to it; to delete data held by Zhipu, use the options in Zhipu's privacy policy.

Data on your device

  • Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on your device, including language packs, settings and your saved Zhipu API key, and stops all further collection.
  • Clearing app data (Android Settings → Apps → [app name] → Storage → Clear data) deletes your settings and saved API key and resets the app's Firebase IDs.

Uninstalling or clearing data doesn't delete data already sent to Firebase. That data is deleted at the end of the retention periods in our Privacy Policy, or sooner if you email us.

Purchases

Google Play handles all payments. We never see your card or payment details. Google Play keeps purchase records under Google's policies; see Google's privacy policy.
